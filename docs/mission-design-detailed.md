# Call of War — Expanded 10-Mission Campaign Design

## Setting & Historical Context
**Time period:** April to December 1915  
**Location:** Gallipoli Peninsula, Ottoman Empire  
**Historical basis:** The Gallipoli Campaign — an Allied attempt to secure a route to Russia through the Dardanelles Strait. The campaign ultimately ended in withdrawal, but the ANZAC forces earned a legendary reputation for courage and resilience.

## Player Character
**Name:** Private John Wilson  
**Age:** 21  
**Origin:** Bathurst, New South Wales, Australia  
**Background:** Shearer's son; writes letters home to his sister Mary between missions  
**Character arc:** Begins as a determined but inexperienced soldier. The campaign hardens him, and the loss of his friends reshapes his understanding of war and sacrifice.

---

## The Squad

### John Wilson (Protagonist)
- Quiet and steady under pressure
- Reflective; writes letters to his sister Mary
- Becomes the emotional anchor after Smithy's death
- Carries Smithy's mouth organ for the remainder of the campaign

### Tom "Smithy" Smith
- Cheerful larrikin from Melbourne
- The heart and morale of the squad
- Carries a battered mouth organ and plays it during quiet moments
- **Dies in Mission 3 (Lone Pine) saving Wilson from enemy fire**
- His death is the emotional pivot point of the campaign

### Frank O'Brien
- Stubborn Irish-Australian miner from Ballarat
- Acts as the squad's mentor and experienced soldier
- Lewis gun/support specialist
- Wounded in Mission 4 but continues fighting
- Loyal to Wilson and Dusty, especially after Smithy's death

### Billy "Dusty" Rhodes
- Young bushman from Queensland
- Natural marksman and scout
- Handles reconnaissance and sniping roles
- Survives the campaign with Wilson and Frank
- Represents the quiet strength of the Australian outback

---

## Historical Note on the Campaign Ending

The real Gallipoli campaign ended in an Allied **withdrawal, not conquest**. The Ottoman defenders under Mustafa Kemal fought fiercely to protect their homeland. The ANZAC legacy is built partly on mutual respect between the two sides — despite being enemies, soldiers on both sides recognized each other's courage.

**Design note:** This campaign reflects that historical outcome. The final mission is not about "taking the coast" but about holding the line during evacuation — a more nuanced and historically grounded ending that honors both the ANZAC sacrifice and the Ottoman defense.

## The 10-Mission Campaign

### **Mission 1: Dawn at Anzac Cove (25 April 1915)**

**Historical context:** The famous dawn landing at Gallipoli. The boats drifted to the wrong beach, landing troops on steep, scrub-covered cliffs instead of the planned sandy beach.

**Story beat:**  
You wake up on a transport ship approaching the coast. The sky is turning grey with dawn. Smithy is checking his rifle nervously. Frank grips the side of the boat. Dusty looks calm but alert. Then Turkish artillery opens up.

**Objectives:**
- Exit the landing boat and advance off the beach under fire
- Reach the cover of the scrub and rocks
- Climb the steep cliffsides with your squad
- Link up with other Australian units at the ridgeline

**Gameplay focus:**  
Tutorial mission. Introduces movement, aiming, and cover mechanics. Players learn to use the terrain, find safe routes, and move as a squad. The chaos of the landing gives way to the struggle of climbing vertical terrain under sporadic fire. Ends with the squad reaching the ridgeline, breathing hard, looking out over the peninsula.

**Key scenes:**
- The chaotic beach landing
- Scrambling through scrub under fire
- Smithy cracking a nervous joke
- Reaching the top and seeing the vast Ottoman defenses ahead

**Tone:** Intense, disorienting, awe-inspiring

---

### **Mission 2: Shrapnel Valley**

**Historical context:** The valleys around Anzac Cove were nicknamed "Shrapnel Valley" for the constant artillery fire that fell on advancing troops.

**Story beat:**  
After securing the ridgeline, the squad pushes inland through gullies and dense scrub toward Ottoman outposts. The terrain is harsh, broken, and hostile. Turkish snipers and machine gun nests are scattered across the landscape.

**Objectives:**
- Move through the gully system
- Clear three Ottoman outposts
- Secure supplies and ammunition from captured positions
- Reach a temporary defensive line

**Gameplay focus:**  
Introduces bayonet charges, Mills bombs (grenades), and limited ammo management. Dusty has a significant scouting section where the player must cover his advance and flank enemies. Teaches squad tactics: suppressive fire, flanking, and coordinated assaults.

**Key scenes:**
- Frank teaching Wilson how to throw Mills bombs
- Dusty scouting ahead and signaling enemy positions
- A desperate bayonet charge into an outpost
- Realizing the scale of the Ottoman defenses

**Tone:** Tactical, educational, growing dread

---

### **Mission 3: Lone Pine (August 1915)**

**Historical context:** The Battle of Lone Pine was a fierce diversionary assault on heavily fortified Ottoman trenches. The fighting was brutal and close-quarters, with soldiers fighting in covered tunnels and over parapets.

**Story beat:**  
Four months have passed. The squad has hardened through constant skirmishing. Now they are ordered to launch a direct assault on the Ottoman trench system at Lone Pine — a feint to draw enemy attention while other forces attempt a breakthrough elsewhere. The trenches are covered with timber and earth, creating a labyrinth of tunnels and firing positions. The assault is chaotic and savage.

**Objectives:**
- Launch the initial attack over No Man's Land
- Break into the trench system
- Clear the covered tunnels
- Secure the main trench line
- Hold the position against counterattacks

**Gameplay focus:**  
Intense close-quarters trench warfare. Tight corridors, grenades bouncing off wooden supports, bayonet fighting, and desperate hand-to-hand combat. Limited visibility and constant ambushes. The mission emphasizes the brutal reality of trench warfare and the cost of taking fortified positions.

**Critical story moment:**  
During a tunnel clearing section, the squad encounters a strong Ottoman counterattack. Smithy and Wilson are pinned down. Smithy, always quick-thinking, spots an enemy grenadier preparing to throw. He pushes Wilson out of the way and takes the blast. He dies in Wilson's arms, still trying to joke, passing his mouth organ to Wilson.

**Final objectives after Smithy's death:**
- Stabilize the squad
- Complete the trench capture
- Hold the line long enough for other units to fall back
- Extract with Frank and Dusty

**Key scenes:**
- The chaotic assault across No Man's Land
- Breaking into the first trench line
- The intensity of tunnel-to-tunnel fighting
- Smithy's death
- Wilson, grief-stricken, picking up the mouth organ
- The squad continuing to fight through their shock

**Tone:** Brutal, tragic, defining

---

### **Mission 4: Night Attack on the Sari Bair Range (August 1915)**

**Historical context:** The August offensive at Sari Bair was the last major attempt to break through Ottoman lines. Fighting in darkness added to the confusion and danger.

**Story beat:**  
Three days after Smithy's death, the squad is ordered to join a night assault on Chunuk Bair, a strategic height in the Sari Bair range. They move at night through ravines and steep terrain. The darkness is both a shield and a source of confusion. Turkish flares light up the sky at random intervals. Every shadow could hide an enemy.

**Objectives:**
- Navigate the ravines at night
- Eliminate Ottoman sentries quietly (stealth focus)
- Use flares and darkness tactically
- Reach the assembly point for the main assault
- Cover Frank as he provides support fire
- Fight through to a secure position

**Gameplay focus:**  
Stealth-heavy mission using darkness, shadows, and silent takedowns. Introduces flare mechanics and poor visibility. The player must use cover and darkness intelligently. Limited ammunition encourages careful aim and melee takedowns. The mission emphasizes vulnerability and the psychological weight of night combat.

**Key story moment:**  
Midway through the mission, Frank is wounded by shrapnel. Wilson has to decide: call for a retreat or push forward. He chooses to continue, supporting the wounded Frank and driving toward the objective. This shows Wilson's growth and determination, even in grief.

**Key scenes:**
- Moving through pitch-black ravines
- Flares illuminating the landscape suddenly
- Silent elimination of sentries
- Frank being hit and going down
- Wilson dragging Frank to cover and fighting through to safety
- Reaching the ridgeline as dawn breaks, exhausted and wounded

**Tone:** Tense, psychological, determined

---

### **Mission 5: The Nek (August 1915)**

**Historical context:** The Battle of the Nek was one of the most famous (or infamous) battles of Gallipoli. Australian and New Zealand troops were ordered to charge across open ground into entrenched machine gun fire. The casualty rate was catastrophic.

**Story beat:**  
The breakthrough attempt is failing. The Turkish lines are holding. High command orders a direct cavalry-style charge across open ground (the Nek) to break the stalemate. It's a desperate, possibly suicidal assault. Wilson, Frank, and Dusty are ordered to lead the charge.

**Objectives:**
- Assault across the Nek under heavy machine gun fire
- Reach the Ottoman trench line
- Create a breach in the defenses
- Hold the position long enough for reserves to arrive

**Gameplay focus:**  
High-intensity assault mission. The player must navigate across exposed ground while taking heavy fire. Suppressive fire from allies and careful movement are essential. If the player is slow, allies die. If the player is reckless, Wilson dies. It's a brutal, grinding mission that emphasizes the cost of direct assaults and the power of entrenched defenders.

**Key scenes:**
- The order to charge
- Running across open ground under withering fire
- Taking cover behind broken terrain
- Desperate close-quarters fighting at the trench line
- Realizing the assault has failed; orders come down to retreat
- A fighting withdrawal back across the Nek

**Tone:** Desperate, costly, tragic

---

### **Mission 6: Holding the Hill (October 1915)**

**Historical context:** By October, the Gallipoli campaign had stalled. The front lines were static, with both sides holding fortified positions and fighting daily skirmishes.

**Story beat:**  
The squad has been assigned to hold a small fortified position on a hill overlooking the peninsula. Turkish forces regularly probe the line, testing for weaknesses. The squad must maintain the position against constant pressure, limited supplies, and growing disease and exhaustion.

**Objectives:**
- Repair and fortify the defensive position
- Repel Turkish reconnaissance patrols
- Defend against a major assault
- Hold the line until relieved

**Gameplay focus:**  
Defensive mission. The player must set up fields of fire, manage limited ammunition, and respond to threats from multiple directions. Introduces static defense and resource management. Quieter moments between firefights allow for character development and reflection.

**Key scenes:**
- Smithy's mouth organ is found in the trench; Wilson carries it now
- Wilson writes a letter to his sister about Smithy and the cost of the war
- Turkish patrols testing the defenses
- A major assault that nearly breaks through
- A quiet moment at dusk, with the survivors looking out over the peninsula

**Tone:** Melancholic, reflective, steady

---

### **Mission 7: The Khaki Trenches (November 1915)**

**Historical context:** As the campaign stalled, both sides dug deeper. The Ottoman trenches became more sophisticated. Snipers on both sides became deadly threats.

**Story beat:**  
The squad is ordered to raid Ottoman trenches to gather intelligence and destroy supplies. The trenches are well-fortified, and Ottoman snipers are deadly. The mission is meant to be quick and surgical, but things go wrong.

**Objectives:**
- Infiltrate Ottoman trenches
- Eliminate sentries
- Search for intelligence documents
- Destroy ammunition and supplies
- Escape before reinforcements arrive

**Gameplay focus:**  
Infiltration and raid mission. Stealth is initially required, but combat is inevitable. Snipers are a constant threat — the player learns to use cover and avoid open ground. Dynamic objectives and multiple routes encourage tactical thinking.

**Key scenes:**
- Dusty scouting the trenches
- Silent takedowns and quick moves
- A sniper taking a shot at Wilson (missed)
- Finding personal items in the Ottoman trenches (letters, photographs)
- Realizing the enemy is also just human soldiers far from home
- A desperate fighting retreat

**Tone:** Tense, humanizing, introspective

---

### **Mission 8: The August Offensive Renewed (November 1915)**

**Historical context:** In November, as evacuation plans were being quietly made, the Allies launched one more offensive attempt. It failed, with heavy casualties.

**Story beat:**  
High command attempts one final breakthrough. The squad is ordered to assault toward the coast as part of a coordinated attack. The offensive is poorly planned and meets fierce Ottoman resistance. The squad fights through difficult terrain, taking heavy casualties from machine gun fire and artillery.

**Objectives:**
- Advance toward the coast through Ottoman defenses
- Link up with other attacking units
- Break through enemy lines
- Hold a temporary position

**Gameplay focus:**  
Large-scale assault mission. The player fights alongside many other soldiers, but the focus remains on the squad's survival. Artillery barrages, machine guns, and snipers create a chaotic battlefield. The mission emphasizes the scale and chaos of major offensives.

**Key scenes:**
- Artillery bombardment opening the assault
- Charging across rough terrain
- Supporting fire from Frank
- Dusty identifying enemy positions
- Reaching an objective and realizing it's incomplete — the breakthrough has failed
- Orders coming down to consolidate and prepare for withdrawal

**Tone:** Violent, chaotic, sobering

---

### **Mission 9: The Evacuation Begins (December 1915)**

**Historical context:** The evacuation from Gallipoli was one of the most carefully planned operations of the war. Units were secretly withdrawn, with the famous "self-firing rifles" left behind to maintain the illusion of a fully manned line.

**Story beat:**  
Word comes down quietly: the campaign is over. High command has ordered a full withdrawal. The squad is assigned to hold the line during the night evacuation, then silently leave themselves. They must maintain the appearance of a fully manned position while troops move to the beach behind them.

**Objectives:**
- Maintain defensive positions
- Repel Ottoman patrols (to maintain cover)
- Set up the drip-rifles (self-firing mechanisms)
- Coordinate with units withdrawing behind
- Prepare for a silent, organized retreat

**Gameplay focus:**  
Defensive mission with a twist: the player must balance combat with coordination. Quiet moments and mechanical puzzles (setting up the drip-rifles) break up the firefights. The mission emphasizes tension and precision — any loud noise could alert the Ottomans to the retreat.

**Key scenes:**
- Wilson and the squad understanding they are leaving
- Smithy's mouth organ played quietly one last time
- The eerie sight of drip-rifles set to fire on their own
- Looking back at the positions they held for months
- Moving carefully, silently, toward the beach

**Tone:** Bittersweet, reflective, quiet

---

### **Mission 10: The Last Boat (December 1915)**

**Historical context:** The final withdrawals from Gallipoli were tense operations. The last units to leave had to fight their way to the beach if discovered.

**Story beat:**  
The final night. Wilson, Frank, and Dusty are among the last to leave. They move toward the beach in darkness, carrying their wounded and the memory of their fallen comrade Smithy. Ottoman patrols are active, but most of the enemy doesn't yet realize the Allies are gone. The squad makes it to the beach, where boats are waiting.

**Objectives:**
- Move toward the evacuation beach
- Avoid or eliminate Ottoman patrols
- Protect wounded soldiers
- Hold a perimeter at the beach
- Board the final evacuation boat

**Gameplay focus:**  
Final combat mission, but with a defensive, protective goal rather than an aggressive one. The player must escort wounded soldiers, manage a static defense, and then extract. The mission balances action with narrative weight.

**Final scenes:**
- The squad moving through the darkness
- Ottoman patrols encountered and dealt with
- Reaching the beach and seeing the boats
- Frank and Dusty helping Wilson aboard
- Wilson looking back at Gallipoli one last time
- The boat pulling away from the shore

**Post-mission epilogue:**  
Instead of a cutscene, the player is placed on the deck of the transport ship. Wilson is alone with the sea and the dawn. He pulls out Smithy's mouth organ and plays a quiet, mournful tune. Voice-over: Wilson reading his final letter to his sister Mary, reflecting on what he's seen, who he's lost, and what it means to survive when others don't.

Final image: The ship sailing away from Gallipoli, the peninsula fading into the distance as dawn breaks.

**Tone:** Melancholic, reflective, bittersweet victory

## Campaign Themes

- **Brotherhood under fire:** The squad is a family, and losing Smithy tests the bonds of the survivors.
- **The human cost of war:** Not glory, but mud, fear, exhaustion, and loss.
- **Mutual respect between enemies:** The Ottomans are portrayed as determined defenders of their homeland, not faceless villains.
- **Resilience and survival:** The campaign is about enduring, not conquering.
- **Memory and honor:** Smithy's mouth organ and Wilson's letters become symbols of remembrance.
- **The complexity of withdrawal:** Victory is not always conquest; sometimes survival is victory.

## Character Arcs

### John Wilson
**Beginning:** Determined but inexperienced shearer's son, eager to prove himself.  
**Middle:** Hardened by combat, united with his squad.  
**Turning point:** Smithy's death in Mission 3. Wilson is devastated but resolves to honor his friend.  
**End:** A survivor haunted by loss, but also shaped by the bonds he's formed. He leaves Gallipoli changed forever.

### Frank O'Brien
**Beginning:** Experienced mentor, stable presence.  
**Throughout:** Steadfast support for Wilson and Dusty.  
**Wound:** Wounded in Mission 4 but continues fighting.  
**End:** Survives to the evacuation, a mentor figure who stays loyal to the squad.

### Billy "Dusty" Rhodes
**Beginning:** Quiet bushman, natural scout.  
**Throughout:** Reliable and skilled, always reliable.  
**Key moment:** Recognizes Wilson's growth after Smithy's death.  
**End:** Survives to the evacuation, represents the quiet strength of the outback.

### Tom "Smithy" Smith
**Beginning:** Cheerful larrikin, morale keeper, the heart of the squad.  
**Throughout:** Always joking, always supportive, always present.  
**Death:** Sacrifices himself in Mission 3, protecting Wilson.  
**Legacy:** His mouth organ becomes a symbol of memory, carried by Wilson for the rest of the campaign.

## Design Principles

1. **Historical grounding:** The missions follow real events and locations from the Gallipoli campaign. Details are researched and respectful.

2. **Emotional weight:** The loss of Smithy is the emotional pivot. The campaign should feel personal, not abstract.

3. **Tactical gameplay:** Cover, movement, squad coordination, and resource management matter more than individual reflexes.

4. **Laptop-friendly:** Missions are medium-sized, not open-world. Graphics are detailed but not ultra-demanding.

5. **Respect for all sides:** Ottoman defenders are portrayed with dignity. The story is not propaganda; it's a soldier's story.

6. **Quiet moments matter:** Between firefights, character development and reflection make the action meaningful.

---

## Technical Notes

- **Engine:** Designed for Unreal Engine 5 or similar modern FPS framework
- **Optimization:** Missions should run at stable 60 FPS on mid-range laptops (GTX 1650 / RTX 3050 equivalent)
- **Mission length:** 20-40 minutes per mission, total campaign ~6-8 hours
- **Narrative delivery:** Mix of in-game dialogue, radio communications, and post-mission reflection letters

---

## Conclusion

Call of War is a story-driven WW1 FPS that follows Private John Wilson through the Gallipoli campaign. Over 10 missions, from the famous dawn landing to the evacuation, the player experiences the reality of trench warfare, the bonds of brotherhood, and the cost of survival. The game honors both the ANZAC sacrifice and the Ottoman defense, telling a human story within a historical tragedy.

The game ends not with conquest, but with reflection — Wilson surviving, but forever marked by what he's seen and who he's lost.
